*Last updated: May 2026*

## The Short Version

Donaurantium collects no data. It cannot collect data. It has no internet permission.

## The Full Version

### Information We Collect

None.

Donaurantium does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, device identifiers, or any other data of any kind.

### How We Use Your Information

We do not use your information because we do not have your information.

### Data Sharing

No data is shared with any third party because no data is collected.

### Permissions

Donaurantium requests the following Android permissions:

- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE** — Required to continue playing audio when your screen turns off. Standard Android requirement for any media playback app.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK** — Classifies the audio service type so Android manages it correctly.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** — Required on Android 13 and later to display the playback notification in your notification tray.

No other permissions are requested. Donaurantium cannot access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or any other device resources.

### Local Storage

Donaurantium stores two pieces of information locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

1. Whether you have confirmed you are using headphones (shown once on first launch)
2. Your preferred intensity setting (slider position)

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings.

### Network

Donaurantium does not have the `INTERNET` permission. It is technically incapable of making network connections.

### Children's Privacy

Donaurantium does not collect any data from any users, including children under 13.

### Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Given that we collect nothing, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely.
